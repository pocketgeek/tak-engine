# Sustained AI production — 2026-09-28

Passive now keeps growing its home army and defenses as resources permit.
Hard and Absurd continue expanding income and production instead of stopping at
the old force and profile ceilings. Real player and unit-type limits still apply;
profile entries explicitly disabled with a zero limit remain disabled. Normal
and Easy retain their existing force targets and profile limits.

Zhon's mobile producers share their build menus between economy, more producers,
and combat units. They now prioritize an opening army and reserve production
capacity while other builders expand. Expansion commitments include build orders
behind movement waypoints: counting only the front order previously sent too many
producers away. Idle troops also clear mobile production sites using ordinary
movement and placement checks. Passive relocation stays near home and never
issues attack orders. Pathfinding and simulation movement rules are unchanged.

competitive balance or performance benchmark. Its army count includes combat
units under construction. Other maps, opposition, congestion, and available mana
will change the resulting force sizes.

Zhon, Creon):
