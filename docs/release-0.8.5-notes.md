# TAK Engine 0.8.5

0.8.5 follows 0.8.4. It adds **Scattered Mana**, neutral mana spots spread over
a random map by its area, draws at native resolution on Retina Macs, keeps the
battle's effects off the Tactical Dots view, makes death cries follow the
original game's rules for what you can hear, stops a queued building's outline
from lying over a site under construction, and adds a Screen Shake option and
new defaults for several graphics settings. The random-map recipe gains a
field, so the network protocol moves from 277 to **278**: **0.8.5 cannot play
with 0.8.4 or earlier, and cannot play their replays.** A server and all of
its players must update to 0.8.5 together. Apart from the new map option the
simulation is unchanged: a game on a shipped or saved map, or on a random map
with Scattered Mana at None, plays as in 0.8.4.

## Random maps: Scattered Mana

- **A new Scattered Mana slider** in the random map generator, directly under
  **Extra Mana Spots**, with five notches: **None** (the default), **Minimum**,
  **Medium**, **High** and **Max**. It adds neutral mana spots, which belong to
  no player's rounds, across open ground. Extra Mana Spots still adds spots
  per player, fairly; the two are separate.
- **By map area.** Scattered Mana is a density, not a count, so a bigger map
  gets proportionally more. **Max** is as dense as a home with six spots; each
  lower level halves the density (High ½, Medium ¼, Minimum ⅛ of Max). Water,
  cliffs, hills and the space the homes and their routes take reduce the count.
- **Fair and reachable.** A scattered spot is never inside a player's home
  area or on an army route, is at least 60 cells from every start and 24 from
  any other deposit, has room for a lodestone, and can always be reached from
  some start. Spots are spread evenly but not mirrored, as the generator's
  layouts are fair rather than symmetric. They get the same ruins and the
  shipped mix of weak, medium and strong sacred stones as other sites.
- **In the lobby.** The preview counts the scattered spots with the rest, and
  the game room's map settings show the level beside the map seed. To make
  room, **Size** and **Players** now share a row.
- **In the map editor.** Cartographer's **RANDOM MAP SETTINGS** dialog has the
  same choice, **SCATTERED MANA (BY MAP AREA)**. Choosing a level on a map
  generated by an older version upgrades its recipe. See the
  [Cartographer guide](cartographer-guide.md#generating-a-random-map).
- **Old recipes still work.** The level rides in the map's recipe as generator
  version 9. A version-8 recipe (0.8.0 to 0.8.4) reads as None and generates
  exactly the same map as before, so saved recipes keep their maps.

See [random map generation](random-map-generation.md#scattered-mana-version-9)
for the placement rules.

## macOS: native Retina resolution

On a Retina display the game used to draw at half resolution and let macOS
double it, so everything looked soft. It now draws at the display's full
resolution, as Windows does with Ignore Windows Display Scaling.

- The option is the same row, shown on macOS as **Native Retina Resolution**
  (Options → Graphics, **on** by default, applied after a restart).
- The first time it runs on a Retina display, **UI Scale** and **Cursor Size**
  are doubled so the interface and cursor keep their size, now sharp. Adjust
  them afterwards if you like.
- The window can be moved between a Retina display and an ordinary one: the
  picture, the pointer and text input follow the display the window is on.
- The places that read the pointer or size the window themselves (the result,
  briefing and campaign screens, the unit-info hover, the text-input caret, the
  window's minimum size) and the hardware cursor were converted too. The
  change was tried on a Retina MacBook, including moving the window between a
  2× and a 1× display.
- Linux and Windows are unaffected.

## Tactical Dots

- **Effects are hidden while units are dots.** Zoomed out to the dot view,
  fire, smoke plumes, glows, particles, explosions, blood and debris, beams
  and lightning, wandering storms and every projectile used to be drawn at full size and
  buried the dots they came from. None of them is drawn on a dots frame now,
  nor the flames and smoke on burning units and scenery (the burning or burnt
  scenery itself still shows). They carry on unseen and are back, already
  under way, as soon as you zoom in to models. Only drawing changed: the
  game, its sounds and replays are not affected.
- Tactical Dots is now **on** by default; see below.

## Options

- **Screen Shake** (Options → Graphics), new, **off** by default: shakes the
  camera on heavy impacts and on mission earthquakes. Before 0.8.5 the camera
  always shook.
- **UI Scale** and **F4 Scorecard Scale** now go down to **50%** (they stopped
  at 75%).
- **New defaults**: **Tactical Dots** on, with **Tactical Dots Zoom** at 0%
  (dots only when fully zoomed out); **Smooth GUI Art** and **Smooth Movies**
  on; **Texture Filtering** at **Everything**. Fullscreen, VSync, Shadows,
  Trees Sway in Wind, Hardware Cursor and Smooth Motion were already on.
- **Your saved settings stay.** A settings file from an earlier version keeps
  every value saved in it; only a new profile, or the **Defaults** button in
  Options, takes the new defaults. See [Options](user-guide.md#options).

## Sound

- **Unwatched deaths are heard.** As in the original, a unit of yours that dies
  still counts as seeing its spot for two seconds, so its death cry, and
  anything it plays while it dies, are heard even when no other unit of yours
  is watching (a lone scout, the head of a column). In 0.8.4 the game dropped
  the dead unit's sight at once, so such a cry was often lost and a later
  sound, such as a Creon bomb's fuse, always was: in the test, 8 of 20 such
  bomb sounds were heard, now 20 of 20.
- **Hidden enemies are silent.** Another player's units make no sound while
  they are cloaked or carried in a transport, as in the original.
- **Silent by design.** A sweep of every unit script found every death sound
  present except the two the original data lacks (`cregatldie1` and
  `zonbardie1`). The Automaton makes no sound when an explosive weapon kills
  it; that is its original script.
- Sounds are local to each player and not part of the game state.

## Display

- **Queued buildings under construction.** A builder's queued building shows a
  see-through outline on its site. Once building started, the outline was still
  drawn on top of the real site and everything near it, so a Zhon Monarch
  hovering over the lodestone she was conjuring seemed to fly under it. A site
  under construction now has no outline over it and is drawn in its proper
  place, under any flyer; buildings still waiting keep their outlines.

## Known limits

- Moving the game window between a Retina and an ordinary display keeps the
  picture sharp and the pointer accurate, but **UI Scale is set once**, on the
  first run: on the other display the interface looks larger or smaller until
  you change UI Scale.
- **Defaults** in Options returns UI Scale and Cursor Size to 100% and the
  default size, undoing the one-time Retina (or Windows display-scaling)
  enlargement; raise them again afterwards. This was already so on Windows.
- Scattered Mana spots are fair but not mirrored, and on maps with much water,
  cliff or hill fewer spots fit than the level asks for (on a flat map Max
  places about 0.85 of a home's density).

## Compatibility

Protocol **278** (0.8.4: 277), replay format **13** (unchanged), generator
version **9** (0.8.4: 8), campaign payload **4** and Crusades SQL schema **9**
(both unchanged). Checked in source against `v0.8.4`: `kNetVersion` in
`src/net/protocol.h`, `kReplayFormat` in `src/net/replayhdr.h`, the map
generator's `formatVer` (`src/tnt/mapgen.h`), the campaign payload's `kVersion`
(`src/net/crusades.h`) and `kSchemaVersion` (`src/server/crusades/store.cpp`).
<!-- LEAD: re-check kNetVersion right before tagging; update 278 everywhere if it moved. -->

- **Multiplayer.** A server rejects a client whose protocol differs from its
  own, so **0.8.5 clients cannot join 0.8.4 or older servers, and 0.8.4 or
  older clients cannot join a 0.8.5 server**. Update the server and every
  player together. (A 0.8.4 peer could not read a version-9 map recipe.)
- **Replays.** Playback requires the recording's protocol to equal the
  engine's, so 0.8.5 does not play replays recorded by 0.8.4 or earlier, and
  they do not play 0.8.5 replays, although the file format is unchanged. Keep
  an old copy of the engine to watch old recordings. Crusades battle replays
  follow the same rule.
- **Maps.** Saved `.kmp` maps carry over, and generated-map recipes from 0.8.0
  to 0.8.4 (generator version 8) generate the same maps in 0.8.5. A version-9
  recipe, one with a Scattered Mana level, is refused by 0.8.4.
- **Settings.** A 0.8.4 settings file reads the same in 0.8.5 and keeps its
  values (see Options above). New key: `screenShake`. 0.8.4 ignores it and
  drops it the next time it saves; it also clamps a UI Scale or Scorecard Scale
  below 75% back to 75%.

No new runtime dependencies or retail assets are included. See the
[release validation report](release-0.8.5-validation.md).
