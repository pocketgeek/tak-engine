# Naval AI audit — October 1, 2026

The audit uses the installed retail build trees, unit definitions and scripts in
both Standard and Crusades balance. It does not give factions boats absent from
their build menus.

| Faction | Available water force |
|---|---|
| Aramon | War Galley, conjured by its mobile builders. The Ark exists, but has zero weight in the shipped AI profile. |
| Taros | Rictus and Lich can cross water; flyers provide another route. Its campaign ships are not in the skirmish build tree. |
| Veruna | Sea Fort produces Skiffs, Harpoon Ships, Men of War, Trebuchet Ships, Transport Ships and Flagships. Mermen also cross water. |
| Zhon | Kraken and Giant Barracuda. The Barracuda moves from Shaman to Beast Tamer in Crusades balance. Swamp Beasts also cross water. |
| Creon | Naval factory produces Submersibles, Iron Clads and Stern Wheelers. |

## Corrections

- Creon's authored ship pad is close to the factory centre. Ordinary mobile
  placement's radius check rejected the producer itself, so ships queued without
  starting. Scripted water-unit production now checks the open yard footprint
  while still rejecting solid yard cells, other units and invalid terrain.
- Mobile Aramon and Zhon producers search for a reachable coastal construction
  site instead of considering only the small ring around their current position.
  Checks include builder reach, depth, footprint and a connected enemy coast.
- Naval producers can be added after land production has filled the ordinary
  factory budget. Expansion builders can look farther for a coastal outpost;
  Monarch and Defensive home limits remain bounded.
- Shipyard planning samples the actual authored birth-heading range and checks
  a route out through the open yard. A pad that fits a ship but traps it against
  shallow water is unsuitable for that ship.
- The AI can commit income to its first naval producer or fighter. Otherwise,
  continuous cheap land production could consume all spare mana forever.
  Unfinished and newly ordered units count against that exception, and the
  existing construction throttle prevents starting a fleet of unfunded projects.
- Small fleets can deploy without meeting a wave target inflated by the land
  economy. Easy retains its initial delay and attack cooldown. Defensive issues
  no attack waves and clears naval production locally around its harbour.
- On maps without mana deposits, the AI no longer repeatedly buys sacred
  lodestones that cannot produce income. Cairbray Coast Landing exposed this
  source of naval and general production starvation.

These are AI planning and production-placement corrections. Ground and water
movement controllers, pathfinding and combat range rules are unchanged. They
do not add AI transport loading/unloading or amphibious invasion planning.

## Compatibility

Protocol **212** separates the corrected simulation from released 0.7.19's
protocol 211. Both client and server must be rebuilt. Earlier tactical recordings
require their compatible engine; campaign history remains available even when
the retained recording cannot be played by the current simulation.

## Update, 0.9.1: the nearest coast, not any lake

A Creon Normal AI put its Navy Yard in a landlocked 111-cell pond on a generated
map: the coastal projection of an enemy start accepted any connected water 256
to 1536 px from it, and a point 1536 px west of one start lay in the pond.
`reachablePoint` now takes only the first ring (of 256 px steps) holding water
the ship fits in, or the ring after it, and the yard site test also requires a
water body of at least 256 cells (`navalBodyAtLeast`). This is our levels'
placement only: retail's site search (`classicplacement.h`, `0x412f80` /
`0x411200`) has no reachability test, so the Retail AI level is unchanged.
Tests: `ai_behavior` ("takes the enemy's nearest coast") and `shipyard_park`
(the reported pond).

## Update, 0.9.2: a door onto open water

A Creon Absurd AI put a Navy Yard at the head of a narrow inlet (generated map
"Intel 9310 Dell", yard at (9016,2392)). Buildings do not rotate, so the yard's
door always faces south; there it faced a 3-4 row shore pocket while the open
sea lay behind the yard's closed back. Ships took a median 330 ticks to get
150 px from the yard (one never did) against 73 at a yard opening onto the sea.
`navalLaunchClear` only asks whether a ship can get out of the yard's own
rectangle, which the pocket allowed, and the site search took the nearest
usable site.

`placeSite` now prefers a yard whose door opens onto water. `navalDoorSteps`
runs a bounded flood from the launch pad, by `navalLaunchClear`'s cell rules,
for the largest ship the yard may build, until the ship is `navalDoorReach`
cells clear of the yard's rectangle. That depth is the PARK ring's inner radius
at its least padding, less the yard's half-depth and the ship's half-width
(7 cells for a Navy Yard and an Iron Clad). The result is the detour beyond a
straight run out of the door, or -1 when the ship cannot get clear within a few
moves more than that (it would have to go round the yard). The search still
stops at the first ring with a usable site, and then looks up to 720 px
further. It takes the first ring holding a site with water straight out of the
door (detour 0 or 1), its straightest site. Failing that, it takes the first
ring holding any door-clear site, again its straightest. Failing both, it takes
the first usable site, as before. It is a preference, never a veto, so a tight
coast still gets its navy. Existing yards are not re-judged (`terrainWeight`
is unchanged). Results are cached per yard type, ship and site for one think
(the obstacle overlay changes as buildings go up).

Measured with the user's setup (Creon vs Aramon Absurd, 15 minutes, three
seeds): the old search took an inlet-head site in every run, and in each run
at least one ship from it never got 150 px out (the others took a median of
330 and 559 ticks). The new search never took one. This is our
levels' policy only: the Retail AI level's `classicplacement.h` is untouched.
Tests: `ai_behavior` ("a yard door onto a shore pocket is not clear", "sites
its Navy Yard with open water out of the door") and `naval_ai`.
