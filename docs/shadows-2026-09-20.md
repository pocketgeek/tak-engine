# Cutout shadows and scenery options

The Aramon and Taros lodestones use textured sheets rather than solid meshes.
Their textures contain transparent background pixels. Our model shadow pass
discarded all textures and submitted solid polygons, projecting the entire sheet
as a dark rectangle. The same problem affects transparent ornaments on other
models; it is not a unit-specific ground-plate exception.

## Retail observation

`python3 tools/re/check_shadow_texture.py` executes original routines from the
local, gitignored `KINGDOMS.icd`, with synthetic inputs:

- The model shadow walker at `0x4eda80` forwards the polygon's texture and four
  projected vertices to `0x5415a0`, with shadow mode 15. It does not discard the
  texture as our previous renderer did.
- Its scanline path calls `0x541c10`. Running this routine with a two-texel
  texture and a synthetic palette lookup confirms that the transparent color
  leaves the destination untouched, while the covered texel writes the lookup
  result. Swapping the transparent color swaps which pixel is preserved.

This verifies texture coverage, not full-game visual parity or retail shadow
darkness. Existing projection constants and darkness remain unchanged.

## Implementation

Only textures containing transparency get extra shadow-mask textures. Their RGB
is white outside the silhouette and the normal shadow multiplier inside it;
`SDL_BLENDMODE_MOD` therefore leaves uncovered ground untouched. Fractional alpha
is retained as fractional coverage. Masks follow the selected player-color or
animated texture frame. Opaque polygons retain the compact positions-only path.
Mask references are cached with model metadata before parallel geometry work.

The options toggle now reads **Shadows** and gates units, separate scenery shadow
sprites, and projectile shadow sprites. Shadows painted into map tile artwork
cannot be independently disabled; the terrain tile format has no separate layer
for them.

Tree shadow sprites use the same two wind frequencies and spatial phase as tree
art, deforming around their authored ground anchor. The direction of the sprite's
extent determines which end follows the crown. This follows our optional tree
sway effect; it is not a claim that retail animates trees this way.

## Checks

- Controlled Aramon lodestone screenshots reproduced the rectangular shadow
  before the change and showed its removal afterward. Scratch artifacts:
  `/tmp/tak-shadow-before.png` and `/tmp/tak-shadow-after.png`.
- Controlled tree frames check two animation times and shadows disabled.
- `shadow_test` submits textured geometry through SDL's software renderer and
  checks unchanged transparent pixels, darkened opaque pixels, and partial edge
  coverage. It catches the mistake of relying on alpha with MOD blending.
- Shadow, retail visual, and COB animation tests pass in Release, Debug, and
  optimized Debug builds; all three local clients were rebuilt.
- Simulation, networking, and pathfinding behavior are unchanged.

The September 25 accelerated silhouette atlas subsequently fixed internal face
overlap while retaining these cutouts. The direct path remains the fallback for
unsupported renderers or exhausted bounded buffers. See
`shadow-performance-2026-09-25.md` for the current implementation and measurements.
